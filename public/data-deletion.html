User Data Deletion Instructions

If you would like to request deletion of your data associated with DigitallyDefined OS, please email:

support@digitallydefined.online

Use the subject line: “Data Deletion Request”

Include the following information:

  • The email address associated with your account
  • Any connected social accounts you want removed
  • A confirmation that you want all associated data deleted

Your request will be processed within 30 days.
